Simple by design

Up and running in minutes

No bank account needed. No paperwork. Just your phone number.

1

Create your campaign

Set your goal, choose a category, and write your story. Takes less than 2 minutes.

2

Invite your group

Share your unique link or QR code on WhatsApp, SMS, or anywhere. Contributors join instantly.

3

Collect via M-Pesa

Contributors pay with M-Pesa STK push — no app required on their end. You see every contribution in real time.

4

Receive your payout

Request a payout anytime once you hit KES 1,000. Funds go straight to your M-Pesa.

Group Chat

📊

Live Analytics

See your daily totals, top contributors, and progress charts in real time. Export a full donation report as CSV anytime.

📢

Campaign Updates

Post updates so contributors know exactly how their money is being used. Builds trust and encourages more contributions.

🔗

QR Code & Share Links

Instant Notifications

Get notified the moment someone contributes to your campaign. No more refreshing — we alert you in real time.

Secure & Transparent
